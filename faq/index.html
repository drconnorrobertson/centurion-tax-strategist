Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about our tax strategy services.

What is the 3-Year Tax Lookback?
The 3-Year Tax Lookback is a comprehensive review of your prior three years of tax returns to identify missed deductions, misclassified income, unclaimed credits, and structural inefficiencies. The IRS allows amended returns for up to three prior tax years, and our average client recovers between $50,000 and $200,000.
Who is Centurion Tax Strategist for?
We work exclusively with high net worth business owners, practice owners, and investors earning $500,000 or more per year. Our clients include medical professionals, attorneys, tech founders, real estate investors, e-commerce operators, and franchise owners.
How is this different from what my CPA does?
Most CPAs focus on compliance -- filing returns and meeting deadlines. We focus on strategy. We review your returns for missed savings, restructure your entities for optimal taxation, and build forward-looking plans that reduce your tax burden by tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars.
What does the engagement cost?
Our advisory engagement is a flat fee based on the complexity of your situation. During your free strategy call, we will outline the expected cost and projected return. We do not take engagements where the expected savings do not significantly exceed the fee.
How long does the 3-Year Lookback take?
Typically 2-4 weeks from the time we receive your documents. Complex situations with multiple entities or states may take slightly longer. We prioritize thoroughness over speed because every dollar matters.
Can you help with IRS issues?
Yes. We handle unfiled returns, IRS penalties, tax liens, bank levies, offers in compromise, installment agreements, and audit representation. If you have an active IRS issue, we recommend scheduling a call immediately.
What is cost segregation?
Cost segregation is an IRS-approved strategy that reclassifies components of a building into shorter depreciation periods (5, 7, or 15 years instead of 27.5 or 39). This accelerates your deductions and significantly reduces your tax bill in the early years of property ownership.
What is the STR tax loophole?
Short-term rentals with an average guest stay of 7 days or less are not treated as passive rental activities under IRC Section 469. When the owner materially participates, losses from the STR -- including depreciation from cost segregation -- can offset W-2 and business income without limit.
Do I need to be a real estate professional?
Not necessarily. The STR loophole works independently of Real Estate Professional Status. However, REPS provides additional benefits for long-term rental properties by allowing those losses to offset active income as well. We evaluate both strategies based on your situation.
What is entity restructuring?
Entity restructuring involves changing your business legal structure to optimize taxation. Common examples include converting from a sole proprietorship to an S-Corp to reduce self-employment tax, or adding a C-Corp for fringe benefits and the 21% flat rate.
Can you work with my existing CPA?
Absolutely. Many of our clients keep their CPA for day-to-day compliance and bookkeeping while we handle strategic planning, lookback reviews, and entity optimization. We coordinate directly with your CPA to ensure seamless implementation.
What states do you serve?
We serve business owners in all 50 states. Our services are delivered remotely, and we have expertise in state-specific tax codes, credits, and filing requirements across the country.
How do I get started?
Schedule a free 30-minute strategy call. We will review your situation, identify the most likely areas of savings, and explain our process. There is no obligation and no pressure.
What is a Cash Balance Plan?
A Cash Balance Plan is a type of defined benefit retirement plan that allows business owners to make tax-deductible contributions of $100,000 to $400,000+ per year, depending on age and income. It is one of the most powerful income-sheltering tools available.
How much can I save with a cost segregation study?
Savings depend on property value and type. As a general rule, cost segregation reclassifies 20-40% of a property's value into shorter depreciation periods. On a $1 million property, this can generate $60,000-$120,000+ in accelerated first-year deductions.
Is the 3-Year Lookback risky? Will it trigger an audit?
Amended returns are routine and do not increase audit risk by themselves. Every claim we make is backed by the Internal Revenue Code, fully documented, and prepared to audit-ready standards. We stand behind every filing.
What if my CPA already did a good job?
We hear this often, and the answer may surprise you. Even excellent CPAs miss deductions because compliance and strategy are different disciplines. Our lookback regularly finds $50,000+ in savings for clients who had competent CPAs. The free strategy call will give you a clear answer.
Do you handle estate and gift tax planning?
Our focus is on income tax strategy for business owners. We partner with estate planning attorneys and can coordinate on wealth transfer strategies, but we do not directly provide estate or gift tax filing services.
How do estimated tax payments work with your strategies?
When we restructure entities or implement new strategies mid-year, we recalculate your estimated tax obligations to reflect the changes. This ensures you are not overpaying quarterly and that your cash flow reflects your actual liability.
What documentation do I need to provide?
To get started, we need your last three years of federal and state tax returns, business financial statements, any entity formation documents, and information about real estate holdings. We provide a secure document portal and a detailed checklist after your strategy call.
