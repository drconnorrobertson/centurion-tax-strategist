The 3-Year Tax Lookback

Our flagship service recovers an average of $50,000 to $200,000 in missed deductions from your prior three years of tax returns.

What Is the 3-Year Tax Lookback?

The IRS allows taxpayers to amend returns for up to three prior tax years. Our 3-Year Tax Lookback is a systematic review of every line item on your federal and state returns to identify missed deductions, misclassified income, unclaimed credits, and structural inefficiencies that caused you to overpay.

This is not a basic review. Our strategists cross-reference your returns against your financial statements, bank records, investment activity, and entity structures. We apply the full Internal Revenue Code, not just the deductions your CPA remembered to take.

Who Qualifies?

The 3-Year Tax Lookback is designed for business owners, practice owners, and high-income professionals earning $500,000 or more annually. If your tax situation involves any of the following, there is a high probability you are overpaying:

  • Business income reported on Schedule C, S-Corp, or partnership returns
  • Rental properties or real estate investments
  • Multiple entities or holding structures
  • Employees or independent contractors
  • Equipment purchases or capital expenditures
  • Retirement accounts that may be under-optimized
  • Out-of-state income or multi-state filing obligations

Average Recovery: Our clients recover between $50,000 and $200,000 from their amended returns. The largest single recovery to date exceeded $340,000.

What We Look For

Our review covers every major area of the tax code that applies to high-income business owners. Common findings include:

  • Misclassified business expenses that should have been deducted differently
  • Missed Section 199A (QBI) deductions or incorrect phase-out calculations
  • Unclaimed cost segregation on real estate holdings
  • Retirement contributions that were not maximized or were allocated to the wrong vehicle
  • Entity structures that create unnecessary self-employment tax
  • State tax credits and incentives that were never filed for
  • Depreciation schedules that use straight-line when accelerated methods apply
  • Charitable contribution strategies that were not optimized

Our Process

Step 1 - Intake: You share your prior three years of tax returns, financial statements, and entity documents. We assign a lead strategist to your case.

Step 2 - Line-by-Line Review: Our team reviews every schedule, every entity return, and every supporting document. We cross-reference against IRC sections that apply to your specific situation.

Step 3 - Findings Report: You receive a detailed report listing every identified recovery, the IRC basis for each claim, and the projected dollar value.

Step 4 - Amended Returns: With your approval, we prepare and file amended federal and state returns for each applicable year.

Step 5 - Forward Strategy: Every lookback engagement includes a forward-looking tax plan so you never overpay again.
